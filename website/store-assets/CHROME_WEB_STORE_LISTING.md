## Short description

LeetCode tracker and helper to visualize progress, build consistency, follow friends, and practice AI interviews.

## Detailed description

LeetAlly is a LeetCode progress tracker that makes your daily grind visible. Track solved problems, activity, streaks, difficulty breakdowns, and preparation-sheet progress from one focused extension.

Build consistent practice habits, follow company-specific question sheets, and compare progress with friends. When you are ready to test your understanding, use the AI-guided interview feature on supported LeetCode problems.

KEY FEATURES

• Visualize solved problems, recent activity, streaks and difficulty progress
• Track completion across focused company and preparation sheets
• See your consistency and daily LeetCode grind at a glance
• Follow friends by their LeetAlly account and compare progress
• Prepare with company-specific coding interview questions
• Start lightweight tracking with a LeetCode username, then sign in for account features
• Practice AI-guided mock interviews on supported LeetCode problems

BETA AVAILABILITY

LeetAlly is currently in beta. Core progress tracking is the primary experience. AI interview practice is available with limited beta usage, and some features may change as reliability and coverage improve.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL

LeetAlly only requests the permissions needed to provide its listed features. Account and LeetCode profile connections are handled separately, and users can manage their linked profile from the extension. AI interview sessions use supported problem, editor and microphone data only after disclosure and consent. On-device voice-activity detection helps avoid intentionally uploading silence. See the privacy policy for details about data handling and deletion.

LeetAlly is intended for authorised personal practice. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by LeetCode and must not be used during real interviews, proctored assessments or competitions. AI feedback may be inaccurate and does not represent an employment decision.
