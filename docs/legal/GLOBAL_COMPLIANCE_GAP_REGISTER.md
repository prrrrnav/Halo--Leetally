# LeetAlly global launch compliance gap register

**Reviewed:** 17 August 2026

This register prevents the public website from making an unsupported claim of
worldwide legal compliance. Laws depend on where LeetAlly intentionally offers
the service, user location, revenue, processing scale, payment model and future
features. Completing policy text alone does not establish compliance.

## Release blockers

- Identify the actual founder/operator by legal name and provide a valid service
  address. “LeetAlly” is currently only a product or trade name, so the present
  operator disclosure is incomplete for many consumer and GDPR notices.
- Decide the countries intentionally served. Do not market to the EEA or UK
  until counsel confirms territorial scope and whether EEA and UK
  representatives are required; appoint them in writing where required.
- Execute and retain data-processing agreements with every processor. For
  EEA/UK data, document the applicable transfer mechanism, transfer-impact or
  transfer-risk assessment, subprocessors and supplementary safeguards.
- Implement verifiable workflows for data access, export, correction, deletion,
  consent withdrawal, objection and appeal. Email intake exists, but backend
  deletion/export and response tracking still require operational verification.
- Verify that configured databases, logs, backups and processors actually
  enforce the published 30-day, 90-day, 180-day, one-year and eight-year
  retention periods.
- Complete a data inventory, record of processing activities, security incident
  response plan, breach-notification procedure and processor breach contacts.
- Complete Cashfree production KYC, tax/GST analysis, invoice requirements,
  subscription cancellation and refund operations for every country sold into.
- Obtain a legal trademark/name clearance for “LeetAlly” and the new logo in
  intended markets before relying on the brand commercially.

## Regional work still required

### European Economic Area

- Confirm GDPR Article 3 scope, Article 27 representative obligations, lawful
  bases, processor contracts, international-transfer safeguards and regulator
  contact details.
- Complete a legitimate-interests assessment and assess whether a DPIA is
  required for voice, code and behavioural/progress monitoring.
- Review EU consumer cancellation and digital-content withdrawal requirements.
- Document EU AI Act classification and transparency controls. LeetAlly already
  identifies the interviewer as AI, but provider-side machine-readable marking
  for synthetic audio must be confirmed where applicable.
- Test the paid service against applicable European Accessibility Act duties.

### United Kingdom

- Confirm UK GDPR territorial scope and UK representative obligations.
- Execute the appropriate UK international-transfer mechanism and risk
  assessment.
- Confirm PECR compliance before adding any non-essential cookies or similar
  storage and review UK consumer subscription rules.

### United States

- Determine whether California and other state privacy-law thresholds apply and
  maintain a state-by-state applicability table as revenue and user counts grow.
- Operationally honor covered access, deletion, correction, portability,
  opt-out, appeal and Global Privacy Control requests.
- Keep the service restricted to users aged 18 or older; otherwise reassess
  COPPA and state minor protections before collecting voice or account data.
- Perform manual accessibility testing and obtain US counsel advice regarding
  ADA and state accessibility exposure for a paid public service.
- Review federal and state automatic-renewal, negative-option, tax and consumer
  protection requirements before enabling recurring payments for US users.

### India

- Maintain a clear notice, consent records, rights and grievance workflow under
  the DPDP Act and Rules as their phased obligations commence.
- Because users under 18 are treated as children for these rules, do not lower
  the age gate without verifiable parental consent and a child-safe design that
  avoids prohibited tracking or behavioural monitoring.
- Verify the required minimum retention of covered processing and traffic logs,
  while erasing other personal data when its purpose ends unless another law
  requires retention.
- Complete consumer, tax, Cashfree, subscription, invoice and refund review with
  Indian counsel before a paid launch.

## Ongoing controls

- Review this register whenever providers, scopes, countries, pricing, cookies,
  analytics, advertising, voice processing or retention periods change.
- Do not describe LeetAlly as “GDPR compliant,” “CCPA compliant,” “DPDP
  compliant,” “ADA compliant” or “globally compliant” until evidence for the
  relevant claim has been reviewed and approved.
- Commission qualified counsel in each intentionally served market. This
  register is an engineering and product checklist, not legal advice.
