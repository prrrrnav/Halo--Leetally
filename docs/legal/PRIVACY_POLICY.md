# LeetAlly Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 17 August 2026
**Policy version:** 2026-08-17

LeetAlly ("LeetAlly", "we", "us" or "our") is currently an unincorporated
product operated by an individual based in India. LeetAlly is
the product and trade name and is not currently a registered company or other
separate legal entity. This Policy explains how we handle personal data when
you use the LeetAlly browser extension, website, APIs and
interview-preparation services (the "Service").

## 1. Prominent product disclosure

LeetAlly operates on supported LeetCode problem pages. To provide its visible
interview and progress features, the extension may read the current problem
title and description, difficulty, programming language, editor code, visible
test output, problem URL, and public LeetCode profile information. When you
start a voice interview, on-device voice detection sends detected speech
segments—not continuous silence—to our servers and service providers for
transcription, interviewer responses and speech synthesis.

We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, or use browsing data
unrelated to a user-facing LeetAlly feature.

## 2. Data we process

- Account data: email address, authentication identifier, display name and
  profile image supplied through Supabase or Google authentication.
- Interview data: problem context, editor code and revisions, visible output,
  speech segments, transcripts, interview messages, phase timing, feedback and
  scores.
- Progress data: LeetCode username, public profile statistics, accepted problem
  identifiers, selected study sheets, goals, activity and interview history.
- Billing data: plan, entitlement, usage, transaction references and payment
  status. Payment credentials are handled by Cashfree and are not stored by us.
- Technical data: extension version, provider errors, timestamps, request IDs,
  security logs and limited diagnostics required to operate the Service.

The extension only activates its page-reading functionality on disclosed,
supported sites and does not collect general browsing history.

## 3. How we use data

We process data to authenticate users; conduct practice interviews; transcribe
speech; generate questions, voice and feedback; synchronize progress; enforce
speaking-minute allowances; prevent abuse; troubleshoot failures; protect the
Service; process payments; provide support; and comply with law.

Where consent is the applicable basis, you may withdraw it by stopping the
interview, signing out, uninstalling the extension, or contacting us. Withdrawal
does not affect processing already lawfully completed.

Where EU or UK data-protection law applies, the legal bases are performance of
the contract for accounts, interview delivery, progress and paid plans; consent
for optional Google sign-in, microphone-initiated voice features and any future
non-essential storage; legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention,
service reliability and support; and legal obligations for tax, accounting and
valid government or rights requests. You may object where processing relies on
legitimate interests.

We obtain data directly from you, from the Google account you choose to connect,
from the supported page on which you activate LeetAlly, and from public LeetCode
profile information when you choose profile syncing.

## 4. Service providers

We use providers acting under their own terms and data-processing obligations,
including Supabase (authentication/database), Google (optional authentication),
Deepgram (speech transcription), Groq (AI interviewer text), Fish Audio (voice
synthesis), Cashfree (payments), and hosting/logging providers selected for the
production Service. Data is sent only as needed for the requested feature.

Provider processing may occur outside your country. We will use contractual and
technical safeguards required by applicable law for cross-border transfers.
For EEA or UK transfers, this may require approved standard contractual clauses,
the UK transfer addendum or International Data Transfer Agreement, transfer-risk
assessments, and an EEA or UK representative. LeetAlly must not claim these
safeguards are active until the relevant appointments and agreements have been
completed.

## 5. Cookies and device storage

The website and extension use browser local storage or equivalent device
storage for authentication sessions, security, preferences and progress. These
functions are necessary to provide features a user requests. As of the effective
date, LeetAlly does not use advertising cookies or non-essential analytics
cookies. If non-essential storage is introduced, it will remain disabled in
regions requiring consent until the user makes an affirmative choice, and that
choice can be withdrawn as easily as it was given.

## 6. Audio and silence

Voice activity detection runs locally in the browser. Silence is not intended
to be uploaded or counted against usage. Short or low-energy segments are also
filtered server-side. LeetAlly does not intentionally retain raw audio after
processing; third-party provider retention is governed by our account settings,
contracts and their policies. Transcripts and interview evidence may be retained
to provide history and progress features.

## 7. Retention

Account and entitlement data is retained while the account is active and for
up to 30 days after account deletion, except where a longer period is required
for legal, security or payment records. Interview transcripts, code snapshots
and assessments are retained for up to 90 days unless deleted earlier.
Ordinary security logs are retained for up to 180 days. Personal data,
associated traffic data and processing logs are retained for at least one year
where required by applicable Indian data-protection rules. Billing and
transaction records are retained for up to eight years where needed for tax,
accounting, dispute and legal compliance. Backups expire within 30 days after
the relevant data leaves active systems, subject to legal preservation duties.

## 8. Security

We use encrypted transport, access controls, row-level database security,
server-side secrets, scoped permissions and usage deduplication. No system is
completely secure. Notify **watershaper9.1@gmail.com** if you believe your account or
data has been compromised.

### Chrome Web Store Limited Use

LeetAlly's use and transfer of information received from Chrome extension APIs
and supported page content complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy,
including its Limited Use requirements. We use that information only to provide,
maintain, secure and measure the user-facing interview-practice and progress
features described in the extension. We do not use or transfer it for targeted
advertising, creditworthiness, lending or unrelated profiling. We do not permit
humans to read private interview content except with the user's specific consent
for support, when necessary for security or abuse investigation, when required
by law, or after aggregation and anonymization for internal operations.

## 9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion,
withdrawal of consent, a copy of your data, or grievance resolution. Submit a
request to **watershaper9.1@gmail.com**. We may verify identity before acting.
You may also sign out, disconnect LeetCode profile syncing, stop microphone
access, or uninstall the extension.

Depending on your location, you may also request restriction or portability,
object to processing, withdraw consent, appeal a denied request, or complain to
your local data-protection regulator. California and other covered US residents
may request the categories and specific pieces of personal information handled,
correction, deletion and portability, and may opt out of sale, sharing or
targeted advertising where applicable. LeetAlly does not currently sell or
share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising and does
not use personal information for targeted advertising. Applicable opt-out
preference signals, including Global Privacy Control, will be treated as an
opt-out request. We do not discriminate against a person for exercising a
privacy right.

Residents of India may exercise rights available to Data Principals under the
Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules. Until a
dedicated grievance officer is appointed, the individual operator acts as the
privacy and grievance contact for LeetAlly at **watershaper9.1@gmail.com**,
India.

## 10. Children

The Service is restricted to users who are at least 18 years old. We do not
knowingly permit or process accounts belonging to children because LeetAlly
does not currently provide a verifiable parental-consent mechanism. Contact us
if you believe a child has supplied personal data.

## 11. AI transparency and automated feedback

The interviewer, follow-up questions, feedback, scores, transcripts and
synthesized voice may be generated by artificial intelligence. The product
identifies the interviewer as AI before interaction. Scores, hiring signals and
recommendations are generated for practice and may be inaccurate. They do not
produce legal or employment decisions and are not shared with prospective
employers unless you independently choose to share them.

## 12. Changes

We will update the effective date and policy version when this Policy changes.
Material changes to extension data practices will be prominently disclosed in
the product and will require renewed consent when required.
